# Social preview card

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Make a shared article recognizable before it is opened; refuse the generic centered-title preview.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm white, institutional navy, civic-blue structure, one scarce signal line, Inter, Foundation masthead, and authentic article media.

**STORY:** Readers identify Plan ₿, understand the article title and author, and recognize Bitcoin Roots at a glance.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A fixed 1200×630 canvas: masthead and large title on the left, author identity at the foot, and the Roots mark in a cool-civic right rail.

**FORM:** Editorial split card, the direct extension of the established blog system; code-led.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

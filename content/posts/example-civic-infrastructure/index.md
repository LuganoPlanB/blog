Civic digital infrastructure succeeds when its public interfaces remain understandable beyond the team that built them. Documentation, governance, and operational knowledge are part of the infrastructure—not accessories added after launch.

## A durable publication record

An article can contribute to that durability when it records:

- the problem as it was understood at the time;
- the constraints that shaped the decision;
- alternatives that were considered; and
- evidence that could change the conclusion later.

That structure helps future readers distinguish a permanent commitment from a decision made under temporary constraints.
